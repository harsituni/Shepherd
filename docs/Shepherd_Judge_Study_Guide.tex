{\LARGE\bfseries Shepherd --- 1-Page Judge Card\par}
\vspace{2pt}

{\bfseries Pitch:} Shepherd is a live evacuation system for Edmonton that routes people to safe shelters on real roads as a wildfire or tornado spreads with the wind --- and keeps re-routing them when roads jam or become unsafe.

{\bfseries Vision:} Static ``leave west'' fails when fire, wind, and traffic change. Commanders get a live map; residents get a phone route that updates mid-trip --- hazard grows with wind, roads close early, demand is estimated, phones reroute away from fire and gridlock.

{\bfseries\large How it works}\\[2pt]
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Hazard:} wildfire/tornado + wind; sim \texttt{T+N}; forecast (T+60).
\item \textbf{Road rule:} close at \textbf{\texttt{arrival\_min $-$ 30}}. Routes: \textbf{ok} (60+~min) / \textbf{tight} / \textbf{blocked}.
\item \textbf{Phone:} snap to OSM $\to$ open shelter $\to$ poll $\sim$2s $\to$ \texttt{shouldReroute} mid-trip.
\item \textbf{Traffic:} BPR delay; anti-gridlock. Up to \textbf{100k cars} via projection ($\sim$2.5k agents $\times$ weight). Demand = pin + zone density + day/time.
\item \textbf{Commander:} closed roads, traffic ribbons, live blue phone routes, cut-off banner.
\end{enumerate}
\end{minipage}

{\bfseries Demo:} 1$\times$ wildfire $\to$ pin near phone $\to$ Play $\to$ fire/closes/reroute toast $\to$ cut-off banner. Reset clears traffic. Edmonton $\approx$ $-$113.5, 53.5.
